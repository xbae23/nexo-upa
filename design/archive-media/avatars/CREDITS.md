# Demo profile photographs

Downloaded on 2026-10-03 from [Random User Generator](https://randomuser.me/), a service for sample application data. These are downloaded portrait photographs from its fixed portrait collection, not images generated for this project. Each original 128 × 128 JPEG is stored locally without modification, avoiding third-party image requests when the app runs.

The labels used inside Nexo (Alex UPA, Mariana V., Ana Sofía, Diego Ramírez, Valeria Gómez, Juan Pablo and their short aliases) are fictional demo identities. They do not identify the photographed people or imply that they attend UPA.

Random User's [documentation and copyright notice](https://randomuser.me/documentation) states that its photos were selected from the authorized section of UI Faces. The [original UI Faces usage FAQ linked by the provider](https://web.archive.org/web/20160811185628/http://uifaces.com/faq) distinguishes mockup-only faces from authorized faces that may appear in live products, and asks for fictional names with the real photographs. This provenance is retained for these demo placeholders; it is not a claim that the photographs are public domain or an endorsement by the models.

`campus.svg` and `robotica.svg` are original code-drawn Nexo community icons. They contain no university seal or third-party trademark. Profiles outside the fixed demo alias list fall back to their own initials.
