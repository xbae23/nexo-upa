# Recursos fotográficos de la demo

Se conservan los archivos oficiales UPA con sus créditos. Los dos nuevos recursos de campus y laboratorio son escenas ficticias generadas para la demo, autorizadas por el propietario; no representan la Universidad Politécnica de Atlautla real. Las publicaciones y Dumps los identifican como imágenes ilustrativas.

## Archivos integrados

- `public/media/campus-ilustrativo-480.webp` (34 KB) y `campus-ilustrativo-960.webp` (89 KB).
- `public/media/robotica-ilustrativa-480.webp` (58 KB) y `robotica-ilustrativa-960.webp` (141 KB).
- `public/media/brownies-ilustrativo.webp` (75 KB), versión comprimida del recurso existente.
- Avatares: fotografías descargadas, no generadas. Fuentes en `public/media/avatars/CREDITS.md`.

Se usan variantes responsivas en el feed y decodificación asíncrona. No se incluye la imagen original de varios megabytes en las tarjetas.

## Prompts usados

Campus: “Create one original, photorealistic editorial photograph for a premium mobile social-app feed about a fictional Mexican university community. Landscape composition 4:3. Candid scene of three diverse university-age Mexican/Latin American students (ages 19–23) walking and chatting on a modern open-air campus walkway with modest contemporary academic buildings and trees in the background. One student carries a notebook, another a backpack. Natural late-afternoon daylight, authentic unposed body language, premium documentary photography, believable faces and hands, finely detailed, visually rich but not oversaturated. Compose with people in the middle third, useful negative space near top. No university logos, no specific real campus, no text, no watermarks, no UI, no recognizable brand marks. This is clearly an illustrative fictional campus scene, not a photograph of UPA.”

Laboratorio: “One original photorealistic editorial photograph, portrait 9:16 composition, for a full-screen mobile university story. Fictional contemporary Mexican public-university science lab, two young adult Latin American engineering students ages 20–23 collaborating on a small real-world wheeled robotics prototype at a workbench. A woman in foreground adjusting a circuit board, male classmate behind observing and smiling; authentic casual student clothes with no brands. Bright side-window natural light mixed with soft lab light, muted warm-neutral colours with touches of institutional teal, convincing hands/tools and physical materials, candid documentary photography. Room context fills portrait frame naturally, medium shot, rich detail suitable for close crop. No text, no readable signage, no logos, no watermarks, no UI. This is an illustrative fictional scene, not a real UPA campus photograph.”
